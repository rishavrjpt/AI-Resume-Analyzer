4. Known Limitations & Error Analysis

          

            

              Header Formatting Ambiguity:
              
Resumes with combined headers (e.g., "Education & Certifications") or non-standard visual layouts can cause lines to be assigned to the dominant classification class or left in unclassified ("other").

            

            

              Taxonomy Coverage:
              
Dictionary-based skill extraction relies on curated terms in skills.json. Proprietary internal tools or brand-new frameworks may be flagged as candidate acronyms rather than verified skills.

            

            

              Semantic Similarity Disclaimers:
              
Sentence embedding cosine similarity measures semantic proximity in a latent text vector space. It cannot and should not be used as an ATS score or an indicator of candidate hireability.

            

          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        
About ResumeScope

        

          

            
Problem Statement & Purpose

            

              Job applicants frequently struggle to understand whether their resumes clearly communicate their technical qualifications against specific job descriptions. Commercial tools often provide opaque, gamified "ATS pass scores" or candidate rankings that promote keyword stuffing while providing little practical value.
            

            

              ResumeScope is designed as a personal resume review assistant. It operates transparently to help candidates inspect how natural language processing components parse their documents, identify technical skills, compare vocabulary against target job postings, and receive actionable, evidence-grounded feedback tied to their own words.
            


            
Ethical & Privacy Principles

            
    
              
  • No Candidate Ranking or Scoring: We never generate a "hireability score" or predict whether someone should be hired.
    • 
              
  • Local & Ephemeral Processing: Uploaded resumes are parsed in memory and never stored permanently or logged to disk.
    • 
              
  • No Demographic Inference: We do not infer age, gender, race, ethnicity, or disability.
    • 
              
  • No Fabricated Achievements: Suggestions point out areas where metrics or action verbs could improve clarity, but rewrites use placeholders rather than inventing facts.
    • 
              
  • Honest Absence Labeling: Missing keywords are reported strictly as "not found in provided resume text", never as proof of candidate inability.
    •
